
# 📊 Excel Vrinda Store Sales Analysis Project (2022)

## 📌 Objective

This project leverages Microsoft Excel to analyze Vrinda Store's customer orders and sales performance for the year 2022. The goal is to uncover key trends in customer behavior, regional performance, and sales channels to support strategic business decisions.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

- The dataset contains sales transactions including customer demographics, order status, order channels, and revenue data.
- It was cleaned, visualized, and analyzed using Excel tools like Pivot Tables, Charts, Filters, and Conditional Formatting.

---

## 🧰 Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Formulas)
- PowerPoint (for insights presentation)

---

## ❓ Business Questions Solved

- 📈 Compare **sales and orders** in a single chart.
- 📅 Identify the **month with the highest sales and orders**.
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Determine **who purchased more—men or women** in 2022.
- 📦 Analyze the **distribution of order statuses** for the year.
- 🗺️ List the **top states contributing to total sales**.
- 🔍 Explore the **relation between age and gender** based on order volume.
- 🌐 Identify the **channel contributing the most to sales**.

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- ✅ **March** was the top-performing month, generating ~$1.95M in sales from over 2,800 orders.
- 🚺 **Women contributed 64%** of total sales, with the 30–50 age group being the most active.
- 🚚 **92% of orders** were successfully delivered, reflecting high operational efficiency.
- 🏆 **Top states:** Maharashtra (~$3M), Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh were key sales regions.
- 🛒 **Top-performing channels:** Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart dominated online sales.
- 📱 Strong engagement was observed from **Millennials and Gen Z**, especially women, indicating a digital-first shopping trend.

---
## 🎯 Outcome

This project highlights how Excel can be used for end-to-end data analysis—from data cleaning to visual storytelling. The findings can help businesses focus on:
- Targeting female customers more effectively.
- Strengthening sales in high-performing states.
- Optimizing underperforming channels.
- Planning promotional campaigns in high-sales months like March.
